Looking at lists

  • Cucumber
  • Broccoli
  • Asparagus
  • Pepper

Turn the following directions into an ordered list.

  1. Chop the vegetables into small pieces.
  2. Place them in a pan with oil and seasoning.
  3. Cook until soft.
  4. Serve with a side of rice.

Inline semantics

We can use strong to highlight important text, and we can use em to emphasize text that needs to be read with a different tone of voice.
